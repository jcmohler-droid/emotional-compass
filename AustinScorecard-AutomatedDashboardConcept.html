Logging Compliance memberships sold vs. memberships logged

Why this matters: reconciling memberships an employee is credited with selling against what the POS actually logged under their name catches under-reporting or misattributed sales before it becomes a payroll or capture-rate accuracy problem — without anyone eyeballing rows every week. Today this check only happens at 2 of the 14 sites, and only for three months of the year. A live version would run it for every site, every period, automatically.
