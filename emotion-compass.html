Emotional OS

Notice · Understand · Communicate · Regulate · Learn

Emotions give us information. Emotional OS can help you notice what's happening, find words for it, figure out what might help, and communicate with someone you trust — on your own, or together with a parent or caregiver.

Simple: fewer words and choices — good for younger kids, or anyone who wants to keep it simple. Full: more detail and more choices — good for older kids, teens, adults, or anyone who wants to explore. Neither one is "better" or tied to a specific age, and you can switch between them anytime.

Nothing here saves or tracks anything — it resets every time you open or refresh it.

The research behind this

Emotional OS is a tool for noticing feelings, communicating needs, and considering possible next steps. It cannot diagnose a condition, determine why someone feels something, or replace support from a qualified professional.

If a hard feeling sticks around for a long time, gets in the way of daily life, or keeps coming back, it may be worth checking in with a pediatrician or a mental health professional.

This tool borrows and simplifies ideas from several researchers and frameworks. The "emotional combinations" in Explore are inspired by Plutchik's idea that emotions blend, but adapted to this tool's own set of eight feelings rather than reproducing his original wheel exactly. Worth reading the originals if you want the full picture.
